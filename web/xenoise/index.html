XENOISE

A tracker-style groovebox for the M-VAVE FM-1. Generate songs, edit them one cell at a time, and play them live. XENOISE runs WND-1's tracker over JIANT FM's sound engines.

Install, try, export

BAY 1 · FIRMWARE

Install XENOISE

  1. Switch the FM-1 on and connect it to your computer with a USB data cable. Don't use a hub.
  2. Close the M-VAVE app, your DAW and other MIDI tools.
  3. Press Install and allow MIDI when the browser asks.

It writes only over official V15 or JIANT/XENOISE. Coming from Felucca, Skiff or another custom firmware? Return to official V15 first with JIANT's installer.

Loading the firmware…

Don't unplug the cable while it writes. If it stops, plug it back in and press Install again: it carries on where it stopped.

BAY 3 · EXPORT

      
Songs, stems, recordings

      
Under the emulator's screen:

      
    
        
  • Export song (WAV): plays the whole sequence once, plus two seconds of tail, faster than real time and without stopping what you hear.
    • 
        
  • Export stems (ZIP): the mix and each track on its own (BRK, KIK, PER, BAS, KEY, SYN) as 16-bit WAVs at 44.1 kHz, all the same length. They line up to the sample and add back up to the mix, ready for any DAW.
    • 
        
  • ● Rec: records what's playing, up to ten minutes.
    • 
      

      
Inside claude.ai, files arrive as a ZIP because .wav downloads aren't allowed there.

    

  






  
Tutorial

  
First flight

  
    
    
  1. Power on
    The boot sequence plays in your theme's colours and fades into the tracker. That's HOM: six tracks from left to right (BRK, KIK, PER, BAS, KEY, SYN) and the rows running down.
    2. 
    
  2. Build a song
    Hold SAVE to open GEN. Turn PRESETS to choose a genre. Leave the rest on AUTO, or pick a progression and how far apart the chords and melody sit. Press OCT+ twice: once asks, once creates.
    3. 
    
  3. Play it
    Press PLAY. The song runs through its form: intro, verses and choruses (each twice), a breakdown, a build, the drop and the outro. Press SEQ to see the parts; in SEQ, press SEQ again for LIVE, where the white keys queue the next pattern.
    4. 
    
  4. Change a cell
    Go back to HOME and press it again to edit. PRESETS moves down the rows and ALGORITHM across the tracks. K1–K4 set the cell's note, velocity, effect and amount. Notes read Renoise-style: C-4, F#2.
    5. 
    
  5. Shape the sound
    ENV shows the actual sound with its envelope drawn on it; turning a knob plays the note. LFO and ARP use JIANT's own LFO and arpeggiator, per track.
    6. 
    
  6. Save it
    Tap SAVE. PRESETS picks one of 14 slots. Turn K1 (SAVE) and press OCT+ to confirm. Something went wrong? Hold SEQ and press OCT- to undo.
    7. 
    
  7. Stuck?
    Hold SEQ on any page for a second: the page's controls appear for as long as you hold it.
    8. 
  






  
The Pilot System

  
Four controls, every page

  
Every page works the same way, so once you know one, you know them all.

  

    
PRESETS
The rows: the tracker's row, a save slot, a settings group, a genre, a part of the sequence.

    
ALGORITHM
The columns: the track. On pages without tracks (MST, SAV, GEN) it moves between knobs, and a frame marks the one it's on.

    
SELECT
Turns what's selected: the pattern, a slot's pattern, the knob ALGORITHM is on, an effect on or off.

    
K1–K4
The page's four knobs, numbered on screen in their own colours. Press the page's button again for its next page.

  

  

    
Questions
Anything that overwrites asks first. OCT+ means yes and OCT- means no.

    
Undo
Hold SEQ and press OCT- anywhere. It undoes one step: a song replaced, a slot written or moved, a change to the sequence. Stop playback first for anything stored.

    
Help
Hold SEQ on its own for the current page's controls.
